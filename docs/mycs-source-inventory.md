# MyCS source scope and classroom adaptation

Reviewed October 14, 2026. This is a bounded teaching adaptation, not a mirror
of the reference site or an import of its linked projects.

## Commitment and previous delivery

In the September 26 class, the discussion called for preserving the teacher's
resources and breaking them into a gradual progression: a working example,
guided additions, then independent practice. September 30 reinforced that
model; October 7 demonstrated the local starters and their classroom download
workflow. The complete resource collection was not delivered by those original
starters alone.

The earlier implementation already supplied twelve original Scratch starters,
including Robot Dress-Up, with Normal, Hard, and concrete checks. Their files,
artwork, identifiers, and existing licenses are unchanged by this update.

## Excluded and outstanding material

- The rest of the external site's curriculum has not been scraped or mirrored.
- Linked student pages and handouts were not available through the reviewed
  public retrieval path. Obtain authorized originals before importing them.
- External demonstration projects, student work, images, videos, and other
  third-party assets have not been copied, downloaded, or embedded. Review
  their own rights and provenance before any separate import.
- The discussion included both Julio's and Jacob's sites. This delivery targets
  Julio's free classroom only. Reuse on a commercial teaching destination needs
  separate permission or a confirmed compatible noncommercial context; do not
  propagate this guide into the general-purpose upstream software license.

Additional lessons should be selected explicitly, reviewed individually,
credited, adapted to an existing course, and tested. An unverified dependency
or inaccessible file remains outstanding, not silently substituted or marked
complete. See [the follow-up ledger](julio-promise-followups.md).
